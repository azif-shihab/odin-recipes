Lasagna

Description

A hearty and flavorful lasagna made with layers of tender pasta, rich homemade Bolognese sauce, and melted cheese. Baked until golden and bubbling, this comforting Italian classic is perfect for a satisfying family meal.

Ingredients

For the Bolognese Sauce

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 medium carrot, finely chopped
  • 1 celery stalk, finely chopped
  • 400 g canned crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

For the Lasagna

  • 9–12 lasagna sheets
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Finely chop the onion, carrot, and celery.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot, and celery and cook for 5–7 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it apart with a spoon. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1–2 minutes. Add the crushed tomatoes, oregano, and basil. Season with salt and black pepper.
  5. Reduce the heat and let the sauce simmer for 25–30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thick and rich.
  6. Cook the lasagna sheets according to the package instructions if required. Drain and set them aside.
  7. Spread a thin layer of Bolognese sauce across the bottom of a baking dish.
  8. Place a layer of lasagna sheets over the sauce.
  9. Add another layer of Bolognese sauce followed by mozzarella cheese. Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used.
  10. Add a final layer of lasagna sheets, Bolognese sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheese.
  11. Cover with foil and bake at 180°C (350°F) for about 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 10–15 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbling.
  12. Let the lasagna rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing and serving.
