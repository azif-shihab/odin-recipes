Cheeseburger

Description

A classic cheeseburger made with a juicy seasoned beef patty, melted cheese, fresh vegetables, and a flavorful sauce, all served in a soft toasted bun. Simple, hearty, and perfect for a satisfying meal.

Ingredients

For the Burger

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 slices of cheddar cheese
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tomato
  • Lettuce leaves
  • 4-6 pickle slices
  • 1 tablespoon butter or cooking oil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

For the Sauce

  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon mustard

Steps

  1. Prepare the vegetables: Slice the onion and tomato, and wash and dry the lettuce leaves.
  2. Make the patties: Divide the ground beef into 4 equal portions and shape each portion into a round patty slightly wider than the burger buns. Season both sides with salt and black pepper.
  3. Prepare the sauce: Mix the mayonnaise, ketchup, and mustard in a small bowl.
  4. Cook the patties: Heat a pan or grill over medium-high heat. Cook the patties for about 3–4 minutes on each side, or until cooked to your preferred doneness.
  5. Add the cheese: Place a slice of cheddar cheese on each patty during the last minute of cooking. Cover the pan briefly to help the cheese melt.
  6. Toast the buns: Slice the burger buns and lightly toast the cut sides in the same pan with a little butter or oil.
  7. Assemble the burger: Spread the sauce on the toasted buns. Add lettuce, the cheesy beef patty, tomato, onion, and pickles.
  8. Serve: Place the top bun over the burger and serve immediately.
