Pizza

Description

A classic homemade pizza with a crisp, golden crust topped with rich tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and flavorful toppings. It's simple to prepare and easy to customize with your favorite ingredients.

Ingredients

For the Dough

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

For the Topping

  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup sliced bell pepper
  • 1/2 cup sliced onion
  • 1/2 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes

Steps

  1. Prepare the dough: Combine the flour, yeast, sugar, and salt in a large bowl. Add the warm water and olive oil, then mix until a dough forms.
  2. Knead the dough: Transfer the dough to a lightly floured surface and knead for about 5–7 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  3. Let the dough rise: Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover it, and let it rest in a warm place for about 1 hour, or until it doubles in size.
  4. Shape the dough: Preheat the oven to 230°C (450°F). Punch down the dough and roll or stretch it into a round pizza base.
  5. Add the sauce: Place the dough on a baking tray or pizza stone and spread the pizza sauce evenly over the surface.
  6. Add the toppings: Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese over the sauce, then add the bell pepper, onion, and mushrooms.
  7. Season: Sprinkle oregano and chili flakes over the toppings.
  8. Bake: Bake the pizza for about 12–15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted and bubbling.
  9. Serve: Remove the pizza from the oven, let it cool for a few minutes, slice it, and serve hot.
